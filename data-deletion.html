How to Request Data Deletion

Screenshot Action does not require users to create a Screenshot Action account and does not maintain a central cloud database of user screenshots.

If you have a privacy or data-deletion request concerning information handled by Age of Graphics, you can contact us directly.

Email:

ageofgraphics2024@gmail.com

In your request, please include "Screenshot Action Data Deletion Request" in the subject line and describe the information or data you would like us to review.

Data Stored by Screenshot Action

Screenshot Action does not operate a cloud storage system for users' screenshots in the current version.

Screenshots and generated files may exist locally on your Android device because you imported, created, edited, exported, or saved them.

You can delete these files directly from your device or use the deletion and cleanup features provided by the App.

AI-Processed Information

When you request AI analysis, Screenshot Action may transmit the screenshot and supporting information to Google Gemini using the Gemini API key configured by you.

Screenshot Action does not operate its own server-side database for storing those AI requests or screenshots.

Information already transmitted to Google Gemini is subject to Google's applicable policies, processing practices, and retention rules. A deletion request to Age of Graphics cannot directly delete information held under Google's systems.

What We Can Delete

Age of Graphics can review and handle information that is actually under its control.

Because Screenshot Action does not maintain a central Screenshot Action cloud database in the current version, there may be no server-side screenshot data held by Age of Graphics to delete.

Deleting Data From Your Device

  • Delete screenshots using your Android gallery or file manager.
  • Use Screenshot Action's screenshot cleanup features where available.
  • Delete exported or saved files from the location where you saved them.
  • Uninstall Screenshot Action to remove its application data, subject to Android's handling of separately saved files.
