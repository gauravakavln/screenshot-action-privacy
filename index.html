Privacy Policy

Screenshot Action

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Screenshot Action ("Screenshot Action", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Age of Graphics, handles information when you use the App.

Screenshot Action is designed to help users understand, organize, edit, clean up, share, and take actions from screenshots.

We designed the App with a local-first approach. Many screenshot processing features operate directly on your Android device. Some optional AI features can send screenshot information to an AI provider when you choose to configure and use those features.

2. Information the App Processes

Depending on the features you use, Screenshot Action may process information contained in screenshots or information derived from screenshots.

Screenshot and image content

Screenshots that you import, share with the App, edit, analyze, organize, or otherwise process may contain information such as text, names, phone numbers, email addresses, payment information, UPI IDs, addresses, booking information, order information, QR codes, barcodes, dates, and other content visible in the image.

OCR and extracted information

The App may process text detected from screenshots using OCR. Depending on the screenshot, extracted information can include text, dates, numbers, URLs, contact information, payment information, travel information, and other relevant details.

App settings and configuration

The App may store configuration information required for features you enable, such as theme preferences and AI configuration.

Gemini API key

If you enable AI features using your own Gemini API key, the key is stored locally on your Android device using Android's secure storage mechanisms.

Age of Graphics does not operate a shared Gemini API key for users and does not require users to provide their Gemini API key to an Age of Graphics server.

3. How Screenshot Action Uses Information

Information processed by the App is used to provide the functionality you request.

  • Read and understand screenshot text.
  • Generate screenshot summaries.
  • Identify useful information within screenshots.
  • Suggest relevant actions.
  • Detect payment and expense information.
  • Detect QR codes and barcodes.
  • Create reminders or other actions when requested.
  • Identify duplicate or older screenshots for cleanup.
  • Detect potentially sensitive information for privacy redaction.
  • Edit, export, save, or convert screenshots.
  • Create PDF documents from screenshots.
  • Share screenshots or extracted information when you explicitly request sharing.

4. Local Processing

Many Screenshot Action features are designed to operate directly on your device.

Depending on the feature and configuration, local processing may include OCR, QR and barcode detection, screenshot cleanup, duplicate detection, privacy redaction, editing, PDF creation, storage management, and other local operations.

Information processed only locally is not transmitted to Age of Graphics merely because the App processes it on your device.

5. Optional AI Processing

Important: AI processing is optional. When you configure and use AI features, screenshot information may be sent to the AI provider you have configured.

Screenshot Action currently supports a user-provided Gemini API key for AI processing.

When AI analysis is requested, the App may send the original screenshot image and supporting OCR information to Google's Gemini API using the API key configured by you.

The AI system may analyze the screenshot to produce information such as OCR text, summaries, categories, entities, structured data, and suggested actions.

Age of Graphics does not operate a backend server that receives your screenshots for this AI processing in the current version of the App.

Your use of Gemini is also subject to Google's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

6. AI Accuracy and User Responsibility

AI-generated information can contain errors, omissions, or incorrect interpretations.

Screenshot Action does not guarantee that AI-generated OCR, summaries, payment amounts, UPI IDs, phone numbers, dates, addresses, travel information, or other extracted information will always be correct.

You should verify important information before relying on it, particularly information relating to payments, financial transactions, travel, contacts, addresses, appointments, or other consequential actions.

Suggested Smart Actions are recommendations. The App does not intentionally perform sensitive actions automatically solely because an AI model suggested them.

7. Privacy Redaction and Privacy-Safe Sharing

Screenshot Action includes privacy tools designed to help identify and hide potentially sensitive information before sharing a screenshot.

Depending on the feature used, the App may identify items such as phone numbers, email addresses, UPI IDs, account identifiers, addresses, QR codes, order information, or other potentially sensitive information.

Redaction is intended to help protect sensitive information, but automated detection may not identify every sensitive item. You should review the screenshot before sharing it.

The original screenshot is not intentionally overwritten by the privacy-redaction workflow.

8. Sharing Information

Screenshot Action can use Android's sharing functionality when you explicitly choose to share a screenshot, text, or other information.

Information shared through Android's Share Sheet may be received by the application or service you select.

Once you choose to share information with another application or service, that application's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

Screenshot Action does not control how an external application handles information after you intentionally share it with that application.

9. Data Storage

Screenshot Action may store screenshots, generated files, preferences, history, and other information locally on your Android device when required to provide the features you use.

The App does not require an Age of Graphics account for the core screenshot functionality described in this policy.

Local files remain subject to the storage and security controls of your Android device.

10. Data Retention

Data stored locally by Screenshot Action may remain on your device until you delete it, use an App feature that removes it, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the associated files or application data.

Screenshot Action does not maintain a central Age of Graphics server database containing your screenshots in the current version.

Information sent directly to a third-party service, such as Gemini when you use AI processing, is subject to that provider's applicable data handling, retention, and deletion practices.

Deleting a screenshot from your device does not necessarily delete information that has already been transmitted to a third-party provider.

11. Data Deletion

You can delete screenshots and locally stored information using the App's available deletion and cleanup features, or by deleting the relevant application data from Android.

You can also uninstall Screenshot Action to remove its locally stored application data, subject to Android's handling of files that may have been separately exported or saved to shared storage.

If information has been sent directly to a third-party provider through an action you initiated, deletion from Screenshot Action does not automatically control that provider's retention systems.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the App's design to protect information handled by Screenshot Action.

Gemini API keys configured by users are stored locally using Android secure storage mechanisms.

Network communications with supported AI services use the security mechanisms provided by the underlying HTTPS/API infrastructure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect their Android device and account credentials.

13. Third-Party Services

Screenshot Action may interact with third-party services when you explicitly use features that depend on them.

Service Purpose
Google Gemini API Optional AI-powered screenshot analysis when configured and used by the user.
Android Share Sheet Sharing screenshots or extracted information with an application selected by the user.
Android system services Required operating-system functionality such as storage, intents, notifications, and other device features used by the App.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies, terms, and data retention practices. Users should review the applicable policies of those services.

14. Children's Privacy

Screenshot Action is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating an account or maintaining a user profile.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

15. Your Choices

You control whether to use optional AI functionality.

  • You may use local screenshot features without configuring AI where supported.
  • You may choose whether to configure a Gemini API key.
  • You may delete locally stored screenshots and application data.
  • You decide when screenshots or extracted information are shared with other applications.
  • You may review privacy-redaction results before sharing a protected screenshot.
