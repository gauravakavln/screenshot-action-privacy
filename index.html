Privacy Policy

Screenshot Action

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Screenshot Action ("Screenshot Action", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Age of Graphics, handles information when you use the App.

Screenshot Action is an AI-powered screenshot intelligence application designed to analyze screenshots and turn their contents into useful information, summaries, structured data, smart actions, reminders, and other features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it is processed, how AI processing works, and what choices you have.

2. Information We Process

Screenshot Action primarily processes information contained within screenshots that you intentionally provide to the App.

Depending on the screenshot, this information may include:

  • Text visible in screenshots.
  • Names and contact information.
  • Phone numbers and email addresses.
  • UPI IDs and payment information.
  • Bank or transaction references.
  • Addresses and location-related information.
  • Order and tracking information.
  • Travel and booking information.
  • Dates, times, and calendar information.
  • QR codes and barcodes.
  • URLs and other identifiers.
  • Other information visible in the screenshot.

The exact information processed depends on the screenshot and the features you choose to use.

3. AI-Powered Screenshot Analysis

Screenshot Action uses AI as its primary screenshot intelligence system.

When you request AI analysis, Screenshot Action sends the screenshot and supporting information required for analysis to the AI service configured for the App.

The AI system may analyze the screenshot to produce:

  • Corrected or structured OCR text.
  • Screenshot summaries.
  • Screenshot categories.
  • Structured information and facts.
  • Payment and expense information.
  • Contact information.
  • Travel and booking information.
  • Dates and events.
  • URLs and identifiers.
  • Suggested Smart Actions.
  • Other useful information derived from the screenshot.

4. Google Gemini API

Screenshot Action currently supports Google Gemini for AI-powered screenshot analysis.

To use AI functionality, you may provide your own Gemini API key ("BYOK" or "Bring Your Own Key").

When you request AI analysis, the App communicates with the Gemini API using the API key configured by you.

Screenshot Action does not operate a shared Gemini API key for users.

Screenshot Action also does not operate its own backend server that receives and stores your screenshots for AI processing in the current version.

Important: When you use AI analysis, information contained in your screenshot is transmitted to Google's Gemini service for processing.

Your use of Google Gemini is also subject to Google's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

For information about Google's Gemini API, please refer to Google's official documentation and applicable privacy documentation.

5. What Screenshot Action Does Not Do

Screenshot Action does not:

  • Sell your screenshots or extracted information.
  • Create advertising profiles from your screenshots.
  • Operate a user-facing cloud gallery for your screenshots.
  • Maintain a central Screenshot Action database containing your screenshots in the current version.
  • Require you to create an Age of Graphics account to use the core application.

AI processing is performed through the AI provider selected and configured for the App.

6. API Key Security

If you configure your own Gemini API key, Screenshot Action stores the key locally on your Android device using Android's secure storage mechanisms.

The API key is not intentionally uploaded to an Age of Graphics server because Screenshot Action does not require such a backend for the current AI architecture.

You are responsible for keeping your API key secure and for following Google's recommendations regarding API key security and usage.

7. Local Device Processing

Although Screenshot Action's screenshot intelligence is AI-powered, some supporting functions operate directly on your Android device.

Depending on the feature you use, these may include:

  • Screenshot editing.
  • Privacy redaction.
  • PDF generation.
  • Screenshot cleanup.
  • Duplicate detection.
  • Temporary image processing.
  • Preparing files for sharing.

These local operations do not by themselves mean that the information is transmitted to Age of Graphics.

8. Privacy-Safe Sharing and Redaction

Screenshot Action provides tools that can help identify and hide potentially sensitive information before you share a screenshot.

Depending on the feature, potentially sensitive information may include:

  • Phone numbers.
  • Email addresses.
  • UPI IDs.
  • Account or transaction identifiers.
  • Addresses.
  • QR codes.
  • Order or tracking numbers.
  • Other potentially sensitive information.

Privacy detection and redaction are designed as assistance tools and may not identify every sensitive item.

You should always review a screenshot before sharing it.

9. Smart Actions

Screenshot Action may generate suggested actions from information identified by the AI system.

Examples include:

  • Opening a URL.
  • Copying detected information.
  • Calling or messaging a detected phone number.
  • Opening a location in Maps.
  • Creating a reminder.
  • Creating calendar-related actions.
  • Opening supported payment or UPI actions.

Smart Actions are generated from interpreted screenshot information and may contain errors.

You should verify important information before taking a consequential action.

10. AI Accuracy and Financial Information

AI systems can make mistakes.

Screenshot Action does not guarantee that AI-generated OCR, summaries, categories, payment amounts, UPI IDs, phone numbers, addresses, dates, travel information, expense information, or other extracted information will always be accurate.

You should independently verify important information, especially before making payments, transferring money, contacting someone, booking travel, deleting information, or performing another consequential action.

Screenshot Action is not a financial adviser and does not provide financial advice.

11. Data Storage

Screenshot Action does not operate a cloud storage system for users' screenshots in the current version.

Screenshots may temporarily exist on your Android device because of Android's normal file handling and because you intentionally imported, created, edited, exported, or saved them.

Screenshot Action does not intentionally upload screenshots to an Age of Graphics storage server for permanent storage.

AI requests are sent to the configured AI provider for processing rather than being stored in an Age of Graphics screenshot database.

12. Data Retention

Screenshot Action itself does not maintain a central server-side screenshot database in the current version.

Information that exists on your Android device remains subject to your device's storage and deletion controls.

Information transmitted to Google Gemini is subject to Google's applicable policies, processing practices, and retention rules.

Deleting a screenshot from your device does not necessarily delete information that may already have been processed by a third-party AI provider.

13. Data Deletion

You control screenshots and other files stored on your Android device.

You may delete screenshots using Android's file management features or the screenshot cleanup functionality provided by the App.

You may also uninstall Screenshot Action to remove its application data, subject to Android's handling of files that you separately saved or exported to shared storage.

If information has already been transmitted to a third-party AI provider, deleting the local screenshot does not automatically control that provider's data retention.
